SYNAPSE ANNUAL FEST DATABASE
Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================

1.1 Purpose
----------------------------------------------------------------
Synapse is an annual fest of DA-IICT which includes thousands of attendees,
various events, artists, accommodations, etc.

It becomes difficult to keep track of data like:
  - When and where will an event take place?
  - How many people will be attending the event?
  - Are there any more rooms available for accommodation, and how many?
  - Which artist(s) will be present at a particular event, and what are
    their contact details?
  - What are the different types of tickets available (Platinum, Golden,
    Silver -- varying number of events included), or tickets covering
    only one particular event, and what is their availability?

Hence it becomes essential to maintain a database for Synapse. It:
  - Eliminates manual data handling.
  - Provides real-time monitoring of event schedules, venues, and ticket
    status.
  - Enables rapid generation of reports for the Synapse committee.
  - Avoids human errors from manual ticketing, which often leads to
    mistakes.
  - Supports quick updates -- last-minute changes in venues or timing
    require rapid action, which cannot be reliably achieved manually.

1.2 Intended Audience
----------------------------------------------------------------
The database will be accessed by Synapse committee members,
administration, volunteers, and IT staff.

  - Suppose the committee wants to arrange food, water, or beverages for
    a particular event -- how much should they arrange? They can check
    the number of tickets sold for that event to estimate audience size,
    and use that to plan seating arrangements.
  - Suppose an artist is running late for an event -- committee members
    can quickly look up their contact details in the database and reach
    out for updates.
  - IT staff are tasked with system deployment, troubleshooting, and
    ongoing maintenance. They need operational instructions, backup
    plans, and support requirements.

1.3 Product Scope
----------------------------------------------------------------
The system will provide the committee with tools to:
  - Create and manage events (music, dance, drama, tech, art, formal
    events, etc.)
  - Manage venues and schedule events to avoid clashes.
  - Register artists/performers and link them to events.
  - Support multiple ticket types and pass models (single-event,
    event-package, Platinum/Gold passes covering multiple or all
    events).
  - Record ticket sale details and buyer details.
  - Manage attendee information and accommodation bookings.
  - Maintain records for food vendors and sponsor details.
  - Generate standard reports (tickets sold per event, revenue, artist
    schedule, capacity utilization).

Out of scope (for initial release):
  - Full integration with a live payment gateway.
  - A native mobile app.

1.4 Description
----------------------------------------------------------------
Synapse is the flagship techno-cultural festival of DAU (Dhirubhai
Ambani University), held annually in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. It spans four
days, typically in February, bringing together technology, culture, arts,
and entertainment.

Synapse features an extensive lineup -- from pro-nights with celebrated
artists to competitions across music, dance, drama, fashion, literature,
photography, eSports, coding, and more, making it one of Gujarat's most
prominent college fests.

Highlights include pro-nights featuring renowned performers such as
Salim-Sulaiman, Amit Trivedi, Antara Mitra, and DJ acts like Teri Miko,
each drawing large crowds.

The fest engages students, artists, technophiles, and cultural
enthusiasts through experiences such as:
  - "Battle of Bands", "Rampage" (fashion show), "Footloose" (contemporary
    dance), "Stage Play", and "Cynosure" (main celebrity night)
  - Tech challenges like "Tech Hunt", "Battledrome" (eSports), "Math-ify"
  - Literary competitions such as "Shailee" (poetry & creative writing),
    "Anweshan" (research papers), "Samwaad" (parliamentary debate)
  - Creative zones like "CosCon" (cosplay), "Conceive" (photography)
  - Genre-spanning showcases in traditional dance, mime, street play
    ("Nukkad Natak"), and solo performances ("Mono Acte")

Synapse is a student-owned endeavor, run by hundreds of volunteers and
committee members across teams such as event management, hospitality,
PR, design, sponsorship, finance, and web -- each coordinating tasks,
publicity, sponsorship outreach, and infrastructure.

This complex orchestration needs a robust backend system to manage
events, artists, ticketing, vendors, accommodation, schedules, sponsors,
and reporting.

2.2 Interviews
----------------------------------------------------------------

2.2.1 Interview 1
Participants : Dev Diyora, Ashish Kumar, Harsh Shah
Date         : 02/08/2025
Place        : Cafeteria
Time         : 16:00
Duration     : 5 minutes
Purpose      : To understand the responsibilities and work of a
               Synapse committee member.

  Interviewer (P1): How is work done as a committee?
  Committee member (P2): Different members are assigned different work
  in groups of varying size -- some sell/advertise tickets, some visit
  other colleges for advertising Synapse, some handle room allotments,
  etc.

  P1: Do you face any challenges in it?
  P2: Sometimes it's difficult to find out who is responsible for which
  job.

  P1: Any other challenges?
  P2: During peak hours -- when a lot of tickets are being sold, or
  many events/accommodations are being added -- the database sometimes
  crashes.

  Conclusions:
    - A record of committee members is required, tracking who is
      responsible for which job.
    - The database must be able to handle load without crashing.

2.2.2 Interview 2
Participants : Dev Diyora, Ashish Kumar, Trisha
Date         : 02/08/2025
Place        : Cafeteria
Time         : 20:00
Duration     : 16 minutes
Purpose      : To understand the responsibilities and work of a
               Synapse committee member.

  Interviewer (P1): You've worked in Synapse -- what can you tell me
  about it?
  Committee member (P2): There are many events in Synapse; I personally
  worked on the quizzing event. It was theme-based, with a Harry Potter
  theme. Gifts for winners were provided by sponsors. We planned two
  levels of quizzes; in case of a tie between groups, we'd use the time
  taken by each group to decide the winner. There are many such events
  in Synapse.

  P1: Do you remember any challenges you faced?
  P2: Some members are stationed at the gate for registering on-spot
  registrations. It gets hectic since a lot of people arrive on event
  day.

  Conclusions:
    - Different events must not be mixed -- their information must be
      stored separately.
    - On-spot registrations can't be closed, since maximum participation
      is encouraged; online registration should be encouraged as much
      as possible.
    - Different sponsors' data must also be stored.
